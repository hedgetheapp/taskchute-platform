Date: 2026-10-18

## Goal and decision origin

The Product Owner selected a two-line Android Today Section header, completed/assigned task counts, a dynamic remaining-estimate summary, compact progress bar, and their visual alignment in the design discussion recorded in `docs/planning/2026-10-17_ANDROID_SECTION_HEADER_PROGRESS.md`. On 2026-10-18 the Product Owner explicitly confirmed the remaining progress metric: **progress fill = Completed Entry count / all currently assigned Entries** (e.g. `4/10` = 40%). A Running Entry does **not** increase the filled fraction before completion; its remaining estimated work may still decrease independently.

This Decision approves the following narrowly bounded **Stage 1 Android UI display-only work**, not the separately proposed persistent Section icon feature.

## Product behavior: configured Section headers

1. Present two lines for each configured Section already shown on the selected Android Today Day. First line retains the existing collapse/expand chevron, spaced `HH:mm - HH:mm` range, title, and D-161/D-165 warning information. Do not change the meaning of collapse, Section warning, forecast or drag/drop targets.
2. Second line ordering: **compact progress bar → subdued Material completion icon → `Completed/Total件` → subdued hourglass icon → remaining estimated workload**. The second line starts with the bar's **left edge horizontally aligned to the first line's time-range text** (not right-aligned). **2026-10-18 Product Owner visual corrective (supersedes the earlier duration-text-width choice):** the progress bar must span **exactly the same horizontal bounds as the first-line time-range text**, from the first glyph of `09:00` to the final `0` of `12:00` in `09:00 - 12:00`. Thus the bar's **left edge equals the time-range text's left edge and its right edge equals that text's right edge**; it is neither sized from the remaining-duration text nor stretched to the row width. The visible time label retains the original spaced `HH:mm - HH:mm` format. Measure/layout from the actual rendered time-range text, so font scale/localization affect both consistently and changing remaining-time values do not change the bar width. Stage 1 has no user-configurable custom Section icon yet; do not render a fabricated icon. Keep the later optional-icon slot/alignment in mind without reserving a misleading fake icon.
3. Count **currently assigned Entry instances** in the selected Section from the Day projection, one per Entry (including Routine-derived occurrences). Numerator = `Completed`; denominator = all `Planned + Running + Completed` assignments. No task-definition counts, duplicate Routine counts, historic placement snapshots, or arbitrary exclusion of a row hidden by the `完了タスクを表示` preference. The same summary remains visible while the Section is collapsed. Move, deletion, lifecycle and canonical reload update the summary by existing projection/convergence rules. Do not mutate Day or Entry state to calculate metrics.
4. **Bar fill**: `Completed / Total`, bounded to [0,1]; `4/10` fills 40%, and **`0/0` fills 100%** (no outstanding assigned Entry). This `0/0` interpretation explicitly supersedes the 2026-10-18 Stage 1 decision and is a display-only completion convention, not an assertion that zero tasks were executed. Running remains incomplete until Completed, even if its time estimate is exhausted. This bar is *not* the percentage of estimated duration spent.
5. **Remaining estimated workload**: Completed contributes zero; Planned contributes its known effective Entry estimate; Running contributes `max(0, effective estimate − elapsed execution duration)` for the active Execution, without changing estimates. For any unfinished Entry lacking an estimate, show the known nonnegative remaining subtotal with trailing `+` (`40m+` for example), never falsely present the known subtotal as complete. Use existing authoritative Day/Execution timestamps and clock projection. Do not add cross-Day Running entries to a Section in which they are not currently projected. Do not exceed existing timezone or Entry identity authority.
6. This is a display-only summary. Avoid new backend polling/background jobs. Derive updates from existing canonical/current optimistic Day data and a suitable UI-local time tick for Running remaining work. Preserve lifecycle/operation retry, D-162 completed-row hide, and accessibility descriptions. Reversible spacing, typography, exact minute rounding and update cadence may be settled by Codex within these semantics, ensuring no negative time and a stable, understandable UI.

## D-180 follow-up visual corrective — 2026-10-18

The Product Owner reported the Stage 1 bar was too short and explicitly required its right edge to reach the last `0` of the first-line Section end time (`12:00` for `09:00 - 12:00`). In the subsequent exchange, the two-line layout was restated and the Product Owner answered `たぶんそう` (tentative acknowledgement). The earlier explicit geometric requirement is the authority for this narrow follow-up. **Keep the two-line layout and the entire second-line sequence on one row where already supported**: `[bar] [completion icon] [Completed/Total] [hourglass icon] [remaining]`. A previous chat illustration accidentally moved the hourglass/remaining to a third line; that illustration is **not** approved layout behavior. Preserve the original Section icon scope, warning, D&D, 40%-fill, countdown and accessibility semantics. If narrow viewport constraints make the approved two-line design impossible without clipping or hiding a required item/warning, STOP and present the conflict/options rather than silently add a third summary line or change semantics.

## D-180 follow-up: empty Section completion and continuous progress track — 2026-10-18

**Product Owner approved a new Material Product behavior change on 2026-10-18**, after observing the Galaxy S23 result of the D-180 progress UI: a Section showing `0/0件` should display a **fully filled bar (100%)**, not an empty/unfilled bar. This explicitly supersedes the earlier zero-denominator treatment in §4 above and the historical implementation/test expectation. Keep the displayed count `0/0件` and remaining workload `0m` for a genuinely empty configured Section. Do not create or count phantom Completed entries or change other fractions: `0/5` = 0%, `2/5` = 40%, `5/5` = 100%. The bar still represents completed vs currently assigned Entry counts, not time elapsed.

The Product Owner also approved removing the apparent split of the progress bar (`---|-----`): **render one visually continuous horizontal track** so that the completed segment transitions directly into the uncompleted track at the 0–100% boundary, **without an intentional gap, stop indicator, or separation artefact**. Maintain the existing track/active colors, 4dp height (unless implementation needs a justified minor presentation adjustment), time-range-matched width/left/right bounds, rounded outer container if supported, 2-line header, second-row order, accessibility and progress semantics. This is a **user-visible Android Today UI presentation correction only**. It must not change remaining-workload calculation, warning information, drag/collapse hit areas, data, Worker/API, Section persistence, schema, dependency, Web/Wear behavior, or production.

**Implementation evidence (not a new design requirement):** SHA `a6d231404e1526832b6d9fe1d653c85555717656` uses the supported Material3 indicator parameters to remove the gap, rounded-cap seam, and stop marker. Focused Android pixel verification passed for an intermediate fill and for a full `0/0` bar at default and narrow/large-text AVD settings. See `docs/CURRENT.md` and `docs/TEST_MATRIX.md` for exact verification and CI/artifact evidence.

**Regression evidence:** `TodaySectionProgressTest` passed `10/10`, covering `0/0` → fraction 1.0, `0/5` → 0.0, `2/5` → 0.4 and `5/5` → 1.0. Focused Android pixel/layout and warning tests passed `6/6` executions across default and 360dp/font-scale 1.3 configurations, including the intermediate active/track transition, full empty bar, time-range width/alignment within 1dp, warnings, collapsed state and completed-row hiding. The old assertion for `0/0` → 0f is invalidated by the approved product change and is not reported as passing the new rule.

## Scope / non-goals

- Stage 1 is **Android Today** only and covers **configured, timed Sections** rendered on the selected Day. `セクションなし` summary presentation is deliberately **deferred** rather than assigning a new product rule or inventing a time-range for that group; retain its existing header/collapse behavior. Do not create new historical/future Days.
- The separate user-configurable Material Symbols Rounded Section icon remains **NOT_APPROVED for persistence**. No icon picker, new Section icon field, Section configuration propagation/history rule, Web/Wear UI, API, Worker, migration/schema, dependency, database write, production change, or Release is approved here.
- Existing D-161/D-165 warning text, warning time precision, accessibility and ability to discover overlap/overflow must not regress. Where a two-line layout and a warning cannot coexist accessibly at narrow width, STOP and report layout options rather than hiding/truncating the warning silently.
- Do not change the Section header's D&D/collapse hit target, autoscroll, dates, historical Section context, Section configuration reconciliation (D-131), forecast authority, or D-178 Review aggregates.
